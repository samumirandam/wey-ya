Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

Summary

Wey Ya! does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline and has no internet access.

Data that stays on your device

The app stores the following data exclusively on your device, locally:

  • Blocked call log — phone number, date and time, reason for the block.
  • Whitelist — numbers you choose to allow.
  • Settings — preferences such as blocking schedules and protection mode.

This data never leaves your device. There is no server to receive it.

System permissions

  • READ_PHONE_STATE — Required to identify incoming calls and apply the blocking rules.
  • READ_CONTACTS — Lets the app check whether the caller is in your contacts, so it doesn't block calls from people you know.
  • READ_CALL_LOG — Lets the app detect rapid repeat calls (urgency) and let them through.

These permissions are used exclusively for the call blocking feature. The app has no internet permission (INTERNET), so it is technically impossible for it to send data to any server.

Third parties

The app includes no third-party SDKs, contains no advertising, uses no analytics, and does not communicate with external services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with the new date. Since no data is collected, there is no need for additional notification.
