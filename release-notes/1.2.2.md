# Release notes — 1.2.2 (versionCode 5)

Notes for Play Console. One section per language (the 5 the app supports).
Play Store limit: **500 characters** per language. Copy the block and paste it into its field.

Technical summary (not shipped to Play): bump `androidx.activity` to 1.10.1 and theme cleanup to resolve the deprecated edge-to-edge warnings on Android 15 (SDK 35). No visible changes for the user. See `CHANGELOG.md`.

---
